About me

Mechatronics engineer, embedded and IoT developer, and a fan of football and music.

I am a mechatronics engineer from NUST, based in Karachi, Pakistan. At Thal Engineering I design embedded monitoring and control products for power distribution: STM32 firmware, circuit design, control wiring and enclosures, from the bench to field trials and sales.

Before that I built Build-IT, a robotics and ML education kit, as my final year project. I like owning the whole stack: circuits, firmware, mechanical design and software. Outside engineering, I enjoy football and music.
