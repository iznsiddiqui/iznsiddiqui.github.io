Trainee Engineer, Product and Business Development

  • Moved PCB assembly from vendor to in-house, cutting unit cost by about 25% (about PKR 10,000 per unit). Set up and trained a supply chain team for Gerber, BOM and CPL based PCB procurement.
  • Secured the company's first purchase order for the module (over PKR 1 million) and prepared technical quotations for international clients in China.
